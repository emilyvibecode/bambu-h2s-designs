FIT
Allow the plate to cool, then remove the hook.
Use two holes directly above/below each other, one inch apart.
Choose a spot with at least 8 mm of empty space behind the board.
Tip the bottom toward yourself roughly 15 degrees. Guide the upper slanted
end through the top hole, feed it in, align the lower pin, then straighten.
See preview.png. Do not force the lower pin if it does not align.
First try a few keys near the board and inspect for movement or cracking.
This is a fit prototype with no verified load rating. Its fit must be tested
on your actual board before the attachment is used for an AirGradient mount.

WHAT CHANGED
Old B peg: 2.4 mm square and a 90-degree upward return.
New peg: 4.8 mm rounded cross section, 45-degree slanted terminal tip,
3 mm centerline blend radius and a 3.4 mm tapered end.
The lower 5.6 mm locating pin should reduce swivel. It is not a demonstrated
commercial cam lock. Main body is about 4.4 mm deep; front reach is 18.2 mm.
Round sections are clipped to a flat printing face and represented in
0.1 mm height bands. Actual print height is approximately 4.405 mm.

CHECKS
One connected, watertight solid with consistent winding and positive volume.
704 sampled insertion poses across 44 cross-section bands, including tapered
pins and the front body. Modeled board is 6.55 mm thick with 6.25 mm round
holes: slightly thicker/smaller than nominal 6.35 mm dimensions.
Minimum modeled clearance is about 0.068 mm. A displacement bound between
samples remains positive, but this does not account for printer tolerances,
board manufacturing variation, friction, flex, retention or strength.
Simulation needs about 6.31 mm clearance behind the board; use 8 mm in practice.

RESEARCH
Amazon listing snapshots showed the INCLY assortment at #1 and the INCLY
heavy-duty set at #5 in Pegboard Hooks. The latter publishes a 5.08 mm steel
diameter; the #1 kit includes peg locks. Neither publishes its exact upper
bend angle or complete mounting dimensions. The new shape is a dimensioned
adaptation of those principles, not a claimed exact copy.
